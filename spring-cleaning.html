Deeper Than Your Regular Clean

A regular clean keeps a home presentable. A spring clean resets it: carpentry interiors, window tracks and frames, ceiling fans, kitchen fittings, and bathroom fixtures, done room by room while you stay put. Popular before Chinese New Year, after a long stretch without deep cleaning, or whenever the home starts feeling tired.

For homes with babies or pets, many customers add a Rotowash Floor Scrub, it lifts microscopic dust out of the pores of your flooring, so floors do not just look clean but actually feel clean.

For the safety of our crew and yourself, personal belongings will not be touched during the clean.

What's Included

Every room, kitchen, and bathroom, surface to surface.

Living, Dining & Bedrooms

  • Surfaces : ceiling fans, aircon exterior, power switches, light switches, doors, frames, and floors
  • Carpentry : interior and exterior of all drawers, shelves, hangers, and surfaces (interior if empty)

Kitchen

  • Surfaces : wall tiles, counters, power switches, light switches, doors, frames, and floors

Common Questions

How much does Spring Cleaning cost in Singapore?

Spring Cleaning with Scrubbys SG starts from $380 for a 300 to 599 sft unit, scaling up by size. Maisonettes add $50, and landed or commercial units require a site inspection.

How is Spring Cleaning different from my regular weekly clean?

A weekly clean maintains surfaces you see and touch daily. Spring Cleaning covers what builds up underneath: carpentry interiors, window tracks, ceiling fans, behind appliance exteriors, and bathroom fittings, done room by room in a single session.

Do I need to pack away my belongings first?

Carpentry interiors are cleaned if they are empty, and personal belongings will not be touched, for the safety of our crew and yourself. The more you clear ahead of time, the more we can cover.

Can you clean the inside of my oven?

Interior oven degreasing is not part of the standard Spring Cleaning package, but it is available as an add on. Let us know when you book.

When do most people book Spring Cleaning?

Before Chinese New Year is the peak, and slots are first come first serve, so book at least 1 week ahead. Outside festive periods, it is popular after long stretches without deep cleaning, or before hosting.

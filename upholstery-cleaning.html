Steam Clean or Deep Clean, Item by Item

Every item is priced individually: sofas by seater, mattresses by size, carpets and rugs by dimension, curtains and chairs by piece. Steam cleaning sanitises and refreshes the surface, while deep cleaning works further into the material for heavier soiling. For sofas, counting is straightforward, for example a 3 seater L shape sofa is counted as a 4 seater.

Curtains are steam cleaned in place using a pole extension steam tool, so there is no need to take them down or send them out. One piece covers up to 3m in width and height, and anything taller is individually quoted.

Upholstery Cleaning is a popular add on to our Spring Cleaning and End of Tenancy Cleaning, and it can also be booked entirely on its own.

Common Questions

How much does sofa cleaning cost in Singapore?

Sofa cleaning with Scrubbys SG is priced per seater: $40 for steam cleaning and $50 for deep cleaning. A 3 seater L shape sofa is counted as a 4 seater. WhatsApp 90716978 with a photo of your sofa for an exact quote.

How much does mattress cleaning cost?

Mattress cleaning starts from $80 for a single (steam), $100 for a queen, and $110 for a king. Deep cleaning options are also available, from $100 to $150 depending on size.

Do I need to take down my curtains for Curtain Cleaning?

No. We steam clean curtains in place using a pole extension steam tool, so there is no need to take them down or send them out. Pricing is $20 per piece, where one piece covers up to 3m in width and height.

What is the difference between steam cleaning and deep cleaning?

Steam cleaning sanitises and refreshes the fabric surface, ideal for regular maintenance. Deep cleaning works further into the material, for heavier soiling and stains that have settled in over time.

What materials can you clean?

We clean fabric, polyester, and velvet upholstery, covering sofas, mattresses, carpets, rugs, curtains, office and dining chairs, and bed frames with headboards.

Common Questions

What is yuu Rewards?

yuu Rewards is a Singapore loyalty app where you earn yuu Points on spend across partner brands and redeem them for vouchers and rewards. Scrubbys SG is a yuu earning partner, so you can earn Points on your home and office cleaning bookings.

Can I earn yuu Points when I book Scrubbys?

Yes. Link your yuu ID to Scrubbys once, and every cleaning booking earns you yuu Points automatically at no extra cost. Prices stay exactly the same.

Can I pay for cleaning or redeem yuu Points at Scrubbys?

No. Scrubbys SG is an earning partner only. You earn yuu Points on your Scrubbys bookings, then redeem those Points for rewards across the yuu network through the yuu app, not in person with Scrubbys.

How do I link my yuu ID to Scrubbys?

Does earning yuu Points cost extra?

No. Prices stay the same whether or not you link your yuu ID. yuu Points are a bonus on top of the clean you were already booking.

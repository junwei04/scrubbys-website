What the Rotowash Floor Scrub Actually Does

The Rotowash Floor Scrub machine works cleaning solution into the floor surface and extracts the dirty water in the same pass, pulling microscopic dust and dirt out of the pores of your flooring. The amount of water injected is manually controlled by our trained team, and the scrubbing power is just sufficient to lift dust and dirt without damaging the surface of your floors.

It will not change the look of your floors, but there will definitely be a difference in the feel, that is guaranteed and proven. One thing to be clear about: a floor scrub is a deep micro dust removal service, not a stain removal service. For post construction stains on tiles, such as cement haze, our Chemical Wash add on is the right tool.

Most popular after a renovation, paired with our Post Renovation Cleaning, or as an add on to a Spring Cleaning for homes with young children or pets.

Common Questions

Is the Rotowash Floor Scrub safe for my flooring?

Yes, it is safe for vinyl, parquet, marble, tiles, and epoxy flooring. The amount of water used is manually controlled by our trained team, and the scrubbing power is just enough to lift dust and dirt out of the pores of your flooring, without damaging the surface.

Will the Rotowash Floor Scrub change the look of my floors?

No, it will not change the look of your floors. What does change is the feel, that difference is guaranteed and something our customers notice right away.

Does the floor scrub remove stains?

No. A deep floor scrub is a micro dust removal service, not stain removal. For post construction stains on tiles, such as cement haze, we offer a Chemical Wash add on, and hard stains like glue, cement, and paint are excluded from the standard scope.

What is the use of a floor scrub if it doesn't remove stains?

A deep floor scrub lifts microscopic dust out of the pores of your flooring. Regular mopping can look clean on the surface, but if you have babies or pets at home, you will want your floors to actually feel clean too.

How much does a Floor Deep Scrub cost?

From $250 for a 300 to 599 sft unit, scaling by size. Maisonettes add $100, and carpeted, landed, or commercial spaces need a site inspection. As an add on to a deep cleaning package, it starts from $200.

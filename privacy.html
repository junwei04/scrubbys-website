Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

This explains what information Scrubbys collects when you contact or book with us, and how it is used.

What We Collect

Only what is needed to quote and deliver your booking.

  • Contact details you provide when requesting a quote or booking, such as name, phone number, email and unit address.
  • Booking details such as service type, unit size and preferred date.
  • Messages you send us via WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok or email.

How We Use It

Your data is used to run the business, not sold to anyone.

  • To provide quotes, confirm bookings, and coordinate the job with our cleaning crew.
  • To contact you about your appointment, including reminders and rescheduling.
  • To respond to enquiries and follow up on feedback.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties.

Who We Share It With

Limited to what is needed to operate.

  • Our own cleaning crew and staff, for the purpose of carrying out your booking.
  • Service providers we use to run bookings and payments, such as messaging and payment platforms.
  • We do not share your data with anyone for their own marketing purposes.

How Long We Keep It

Kept only as long as needed.

  • Booking and contact records are kept for as long as needed to provide our service and meet accounting or legal requirements.

Your Rights

You can ask us about your data at any time.

  • You can ask us what personal data we hold about you, request a correction, or ask us to delete it, subject to any legal or accounting requirements to retain records.
  • To make a request, contact us at the details below.

Questions about this policy or your data? WhatsApp or email us anytime.

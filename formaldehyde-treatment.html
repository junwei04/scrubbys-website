Formaldehyde Treatment in Singapore

That new renovation smell is not harmless. New carpentry, paint, and renovation materials release formaldehyde and VOCs that can affect indoor air quality for months, especially concerning with young children or elderly family members at home.

Scrubbys SG treats your home and takes air quality readings before and after, so you see the actual numbers improve, not just take our word for it. From $288, NEA licensed, rated 4.9 stars across 119 Google reviews.

Measured Before, Measured After

Every treatment includes a pre treatment air quality reading, the treatment application itself, and a post treatment reading. We work towards a post treatment formaldehyde level below 0.1mg/m3 to the best of our ability. Results depend on factors such as air flow, the amount of carpentry, when fabrication was done, the type of laminate or wood, and activities in or near your unit, and levels are expected to fluctuate slightly after treatment.

Timing matters: Formaldehyde Treatment can only be performed once your renovation is fully completed and cleaning has been done. That is why most customers book it together with our Post Renovation Cleaning, treatment follows right after the clean, before furniture and family move in.

A pre inspection fee of $50 applies, waiveable depending on scope. A Deodorising Service is also available as an add on from $300.

Common Questions

How much does Formaldehyde Treatment cost in Singapore?

Formaldehyde Treatment with Scrubbys SG starts from $288 for a 300 to 799 sft unit, scaling up by size. A $50 pre inspection fee applies, waiveable depending on scope. Landed and commercial units require a site inspection.

Do I need Formaldehyde Treatment after renovation?

Formaldehyde is released by new furniture, paint, and renovation materials, and can affect indoor air quality for months. We recommend it for any newly renovated home, especially with babies, young children, or elderly family members moving in.

When can the treatment be done?

Only once your renovation is fully completed and cleaning has been done. Most customers book it right after our Post Renovation Cleaning, before moving furniture in.

How do I know the treatment actually worked?

Every treatment includes an air quality reading before and after, so you see the actual numbers improve. We work towards a post treatment formaldehyde level below 0.1mg/m3 to the best of our ability.

Will formaldehyde levels stay down after treatment?

Levels are expected to fluctuate slightly after treatment, though not significantly. Results depend on factors like air flow, the amount of carpentry, when fabrication was done, and the type of laminate or wood in your home.

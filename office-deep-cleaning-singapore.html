Quoted After a Site Inspection

Every office is different, so we do not quote offices from a price list. We inspect the space first, agree the scope with you, and then give you a fixed quote. WhatsApp us your address, the approximate size of the office, and what you would like cleaned.

Office chairs and carpets can be added to the same visit. Office chairs are $55 each for steam cleaning or $85 for deep cleaning, and carpets start from $60. See Upholstery Cleaning and Carpet Cleaning for the full prices.

Disinfection with High Pressure Steam

We use high pressure steam for disinfecting and sanitising. It refreshes and sanitises surfaces and fabric. Steam sanitises, it does not extract dirt, so heavily soiled areas are discussed and scoped on the site visit.

Please note that we do not clean the interior of desks, cabinets, or other carpentry that still holds belongings. These need to be emptied by your team first.

Common Questions

How is office deep cleaning priced in Singapore?

Office and other commercial units are quoted after a site inspection, because price depends on the size, layout, and scope of the job. WhatsApp 90716978 to arrange an inspection and receive a quote.

Do you offer office disinfection?

Yes. We offer office deep cleaning and disinfection. We use high pressure steam for disinfecting and sanitising. The exact scope for your office is agreed after the site inspection.

Can you clean office chairs and carpets?

Yes. Office chairs are $55 each for steam cleaning or $85 for deep cleaning, and dining or meeting chairs are $25 or $30. Carpets are priced by size from $60. A minimum charge of $120 applies to standalone upholstery jobs.

Is Scrubbys SG licensed to clean commercial spaces?

Scrubbys SG is NEA licensed and bizSAFE Level 3 certified, and has been Singapore owned and operated since 2022.

Do you clean the inside of desks and cabinets?

Only if they have been emptied by your team first. We do not clean the interiors of carpentry that still holds belongings.

Why a Post Renovation Clean Is Different

A renovation leaves behind more than visible mess. Fine dust from hacking, sanding, and drilling settles into every drawer, hinge, and window track, and general contractor cleaning only skims the surface. We advise having your contractor's general wash done first, then bringing us in for the detailed work: interiors of carpentry, window tracks and frames, kitchen fittings, and bathroom fixtures, room by room.

If your renovation was major, where most of the previous fixtures such as windows, carpentry, or toilets were replaced, this is the right service. If your unit had little to no renovation and existing fixtures were retained, our Pre Move In Cleaning is the better fit.

Many customers pair this with a Rotowash Floor Scrub to lift fine dust out of floor pores, or a Formaldehyde Treatment to clear the chemical smell new carpentry and paint release.

Common Questions

How much does Post Renovation Cleaning cost in Singapore?

Post Renovation Cleaning with Scrubbys SG starts from $350 for a 300 to 599 sft unit, scaling up by size. Maisonettes add $50, and landed or commercial units require a site inspection. WhatsApp 90716978 with your home size for an exact quote.

When should I book my post renovation clean?

Once your renovation is fully completed and your contractor has done their general wash. We advise booking at least 1 week ahead of your preferred date, since slots are first come first serve.

Do you remove cement, paint, or putty stains?

Hard stain removal is not part of the standard package, but we offer a Chemical Wash add on for post construction stains on tiles, quoted after photos. It targets residue such as cement haze left behind after renovation work.

What is the difference between Post Reno Clean and Pre Move In Clean?

Post Reno Clean is for units that went through a major renovation, where most of what the previous owner left behind, such as windows, carpentry, or toilets, was removed and replaced. Pre Move In Clean is for the opposite situation, where little to no renovation was done and existing fixtures are retained.

Should I add Formaldehyde Treatment after renovation?

New furniture, paint, and renovation materials release formaldehyde that can affect indoor air quality for months. Our treatment starts from $288 and includes pre and post treatment air quality readings. It can only be performed once renovation is fully completed and cleaning has been done.
